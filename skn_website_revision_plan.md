# Shanti Kala Nikketan Website — Comprehensive Revision & Action Items

## 1. Global Navigation, Branding & Layout

### Header & Navigation Structure

* **Navbar Ordering:** Reorder the primary navigation links to follow user discovery flow: **Academy → Lineage → Curriculum → Locations → Events**. This ensures visitors exploring batch links from the curriculum arrive naturally at the corresponding venue page.

* **Menu Orientation:** Test the visual balance of placing navigation items as a side menu rather than the default top-center row.

* **Home Navigation:** Evaluate whether an explicit "Home" navigation label is necessary or if clicking the academy logo is sufficiently clear to visitors.

* **Scroll Indicator:** Redesign and enlarge the vertical `"SCROLL"` indicator on the hero view (e.g., using a pronounced cylindrical slider or pill shape) to make scrolling affordance obvious.

### Branding & Identity

* **Logo & Preloader:** Replace all placeholder loading animations and header marks with the authentic, high-resolution Shanti Kala Nikketan emblem in the approved dark blue color scheme.

* **Emblem Section ("Read the Mark"):** Retain the spiritual symbology write-up (Lord Ganesha, the three interwoven circles, open palms, enclosing circle) while swapping in the authentic emblem graphic.

### Global CTA & Button Consistency

* **Button Standardization:** Establish a single, consistent primary Call-to-Action across every page ("Book trial" in the header).

* **Clutter Elimination:** Remove duplicate secondary CTAs that clutter content views, such as redundant "Schedule a trial session" and "Book trial" buttons appearing simultaneously on the same screen.

* **Label Simplification:** Simplify inline directional buttons (e.g., change `"The academy →"` to `"Academy →"`).

* **Mid-Page Redundancy:** Remove redundant link buttons that duplicate existing navbar destinations (e.g., `"Our lineage →"`, `"The six levels →"`, `"Where we teach"`, and `"Events & gallery →"`).

### Statistics / Metrics Block

* **Deduplication:** Remove the repeated numeric stats block (Years of Teaching, Students Taught, Batches, Stages Performed) appearing at the bottom of multiple pages.

* **Student Count:** Permanently remove the metric `"324 Students Taught"` as exact student headcounts are not formally tracked.

* **Batch Update:** Adjust weekly batches from **16** to **15**.

* **Country Count:** Adjust countries from **2** to **3** to reflect students across India, Canada, and online learners in the United States.

### Global Footer & Contact Information

* **Physical Addresses:** Remove exact physical residential/branch addresses (e.g., Prestige Courtyards) from the public footer to prevent landlord and property issues.

* **Phone Numbers:** Add a dedicated Canadian contact phone number alongside the primary Indian phone number.

* **Social Media:** Embed official brand vector icons/links for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube within a fixed footer.

* **Footer Subtext:** Standardize the footer tagline across all pages to: *"Bharatanatyam, Kalakshetra Bani since 2009"*.
