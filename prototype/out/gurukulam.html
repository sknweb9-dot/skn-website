How the learning works

Seven stages, from a four-year-old’s first shloka to the night before an Arangetram.

  1. Introductory Stage

    Ages 4–6

    A gentle introduction to rhythm, movement and expression through shlokas, playful adavus and creative learning that builds comfort with the art form.

  2. Foundation Stage

    Level 1 · Ages 6+

    Building strong roots in Bharatanatyam through basic adavus, rhythm awareness, foundational theory and simple abhinaya.

  3. Strengthening Stage

    Level 2

    Developing stability and control through advanced adavus, deeper theory understanding and introduction to structured repertoire.

  4. Transition Stage

    Level 3

    Moving into classical repertoire with Alarippu, Jatiswaram and Shabdam, while beginning to understand the history and context of Bharatanatyam.

  5. Expression Stage

    Level 4

    Expanding emotional depth through Keerthanams, Bhajans and Thillana, along with exposure to the stories of dance and culture.

  6. Maturity Stage

    Level 5

    Refining expressive and technical mastery through Varnam and Padam, with deeper exploration of abhinaya and composers.

  7. Performance Readiness Stage

    Level 6

    The final stage of preparation towards Arangetram — stamina, refinement, confidence and independent stage presence.

Theory is taught, not assumed

Alongside movement, students learn the विनियोग viniyoga — what each gesture is used to say. The academy has published lessons on eight of the twenty-eight single-hand gestures, with the Sanskrit verse and its word-by-word meaning.
