Udaan
"Bharatanatyam is a beautiful and intense dance form, believed to be passed on to humans from the Gods! While mastering the art-form requires a 'life-time', every victory in one's path to learning the art should be celebrated. For us at Shanti Kala Nikketan, Bharatanatyam is a Sadhana that helps in the overall development of one's character to be a creative and empathetic human being. Every dancer deserves a platform to perform and express themselves, irrespective of their stage of learning! We are delighted to present "Udaan" as a humble offering to the art-form and as a platform for our dancers at Shanti Kala Nikketan to celebrate their accomplishment of moving one more step closer to learning the art!
​
"Udaan" has been chosen as the theme of our performance to signify "Flying high to reach our goals". In one's journey in learning Bharatanatyam, every dancer encounters numerous challenges. These challenges are overcome with more ease in the presence of a Guru. Like a swan (Hamsa) meticulously separates the milk from a mixture of milk and water, the guiding light of a Guru enables a Sishya to separate and experience the beauty of the art while overcoming challenges, experience freedom to explore and thereby fly to great heights. Our dancers are ready to enthrall you with their dance! Please have a look at their performance.

Our Founder
Om Guru Om
Every art reveres him in all forms he who manifests in various forms, he adores the wise and righteous, and is adored by the good. Ever righteous, he leads people towards the path of Gyan and Bhakti ( Prem ), sheltering all the living beings. He is a Guide to all, he is of infinite names, the Guru - Om Guru Om, Our Founder. For him, Art is essentially an affirmation, benediction or reverence of the existence - the Divinity. The beautiful experience of every art form is that it takes you closer to God and merge with it. This is the fundamental emotion in the cradle of every art. Soul of the art represents the artist himself through which we can seek the god in oneself and others. When art becomes a part of our life.... it takes you to the highest formless form of happiness. Thus every art form is a portrayal of God.
Art is not common
its the divine expression
which reveals itself.
The formless takes form
and reaches to us all within the Arts
Our Co-founder & Director
Sunitta Menghanaani
Sunitta Menghanaani, a profound Bharatanatyam artiste and teacher, hails from Pune, but has made her karmabhumi - Chennai. She began her journey into dance at the tender age of 3 under Smt Geeta Nair. The urge to follow her passion, brought her down South and she graduated in Bharatanatyam from Kalakshetra Foundation, Rukmini Devi College of Fine Arts, Chennai. She holds Bachelor in Commerce from Pune University and has completed her Masters in Fine Arts from Kalai Kaveri College of Fine Arts, Trichy. She hold Best Performer Award from renowned Sri Parthasarthy Swamy Sabha. Recently she was awarded the Exemplar Award from the Dorai Foundation, Chennai. Sunitta started dissemination of her artistic knowledge with the firm belief that everyone can learn dance, and the only talent required is a talent to work hard.

Introductory Stage
(Ages 4 - 6)
A gentle introduction to rhythm, movement, and expression through shlokas, playful adavus, and creative learning that builds comfort with the art form.
Gurukulam
Learn... Grow... Spread!
The Six Stages of Learning
Foundation Stage (Level 1)
(Ages 6 +)
Building strong roots in Bharatanatyam through basic adavus, rhythm awareness, foundational theory, and simple abhinaya.
Strengthening Stage
(Level 2)
Developing stability and control through advanced adavus, deeper theory understanding, and introduction to structured repertoire.
Transition Stage
(Level 3)
Moving into classical repertoire with Alarippu, Jatiswaram, and Shabdam, while beginning to understand the history and context of Bharatanatyam.
Expression Stage
(Level 4)
Expanding emotional depth through Keerthanams, Bhajans, and Thillana, along with exposure to stories of dance and culture.
Maturity Stage
(Level 5)
Refining expressive and technical mastery through Varnam and Padam, with deeper exploration of abhinaya and composers.
Performance Readiness Stage
(Level 6)
Final stage of preparation towards Arangetram - focused on stamina, refinement, confidence, and independent stage presence.

Shanti Kala Nikketan
Academy of Fine Arts
Shanti Kala Nikketan, the organisation took birth on 18th April 2009. Artistic Director Sunitta Menghanaani draws her inspiration from the Founder Om - Guru - Om's vision for a cultural renaissance. Shanti Kala Nikketan nurtures Gurukulam style of education where the students practice Classical art form along with the much-needed infusion of literature and mythology, to assist in holistic development. The Kalakshetra style of Bharatanatyam is the adopted method of dance modality. We insists on developing a synergy of commitment and perseverance where both the Guru and Shishya flourish together.
​
We have started our new branch at Canada. Individual and group classes are happening @ Scarborough – Morningside & Finch . For enquiry of classes please write to shantikalanikketan@gmail.com
We offer both individual and group classes. Join us at Shanti Kala Nikketan, where the arts come alive, and the spirit of creativity thrives. Explore the world of fine arts and embark on a journey of self-discovery with us.

Our Team
S. Kirusanthini
Head, Canada Branch
S. Kirusanthini is a Bharatanatyam dancer, choreographer, and teacher with strong artistic roots in Sri Lanka. She began her training in the Vazhuvoor style under Smt. Sangeetha Karthikeyan and later studied the Kalakshetra style for over seven years under Miss Sunitta Menghanaani.
​
She holds Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Computer Applications and has completed First-Class Grade Levels in Bharatanatyam from Annamalai University, Chidambaram.
​
Her teaching experience includes roles at AIMA, Chennai, and SEED, a community development NGO in Sri Lanka.
​
Honoured with the Best Dancer Award by UNIPUN Sri Lanka, Kirusanthini continues to inspire students through her dedication to preserving and promoting the rich tradition of Indian classical dance.
Tejaswi J
Senior Associate
Tejaswi J is a Bharatanatyam dancer, performer, and teacher based in Chennai. She began her Bharatanatyam journey in her late teens and, through dedication and perseverance, has built a strong foundation in the art form.
​
She holds a First Class Diploma from Rukmini Devi College of Fine Arts, Kalakshetra Foundation, and a Master’s Degree (M.A. Bharatanatyam) from Tamil Nadu Dr. J. Jayalalithaa Music and Fine Arts University. Under the guidance of Smt. Vidya Girish, she continues to deepen her artistic practice.
​
Tejaswi has performed at various cultural and devotional platforms, including Guruvayur, temple festivals, and productions at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Mylapore. With two years of teaching experience, she is committed to nurturing discipline, confidence, and a genuine love for Bharatanatyam while inspiring students to uphold its rich traditions.
