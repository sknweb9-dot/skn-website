## 2. Detailed Gesture Descriptions

 1. **Patāka (पताक)**

    * *Formation:* All four fingers straight and pressed together; thumb bent slightly to touch the side of the index finger.

    * *Significance:* Clouds, forest, night, river, open door, blessing.

 2. **Tripatāka (त्रिपताक)**

    * *Formation:* Starting from Patāka, the ring finger is bent down at the middle joint.

    * *Significance:* Crown, tree, thunderbolt/Vajra, Indra, raising a flame.

 3. **Ardhapatāka (अर्धपताक)**

    * *Formation:* Starting from Tripatāka, the little finger is also bent down along with the ring finger.

    * *Significance:* Tender leaves, riverbank, knife, two objects together.

 4. **Kartarīmukha (कर्तरीमुख)**

    * *Formation:* Little finger and ring finger touch the thumb; index finger and middle finger are extended apart in a scissor shape.

    * *Significance:* Separation of lovers, lightning, corner of an eye, falling.

 5. **Mayūra (मयूर)**

    * *Formation:* Ring finger touches the tip of the thumb; the remaining three fingers are held upright.

    * *Significance:* Peacock's neck, applying tilak/vermilion, dispersing hair, wiping tears.

 6. **Ardhacandra (अर्धचन्द्र)**

    * *Formation:* Four fingers straight and touching; thumb extended straight outward to form a right angle or crescent curve.

    * *Significance:* The crescent moon, holding the neck, waist, prayer platter.

 7. **Arāla (अराल)**

    * *Formation:* From Patāka, the index finger is curved inward at the base joint.

    * *Significance:* Drinking poison/nectar, violent wind, blessing.

 8. **Śukatuṇḍa (शुकतुण्ड)**

    * *Formation:* From Arāla, the ring finger is also curved inward.

    * *Significance:* Parrot’s beak, shooting an arrow, mystery, defiance.

 9. **Muṣṭi (मुष्टि)**

    * *Formation:* Four fingers curled into the palm; thumb rests over the fingers (a tight fist).

    * *Significance:* Holding weapons, steadfastness, boxing, wrestling.

10. **Śikhara (शिखर)**

    * *Formation:* From Muṣṭi, the thumb is extended vertically upward.

    * *Significance:* Bow of Kama/Shiva, pillar, tooth, questioning "who?".

11. **Kapittha (कपित्थ)**

    * *Formation:* From Śikhara, the index finger bends down and presses the tip of the thumb.

    * *Significance:* Goddess Lakshmi/Saraswati, holding cymbals, churning milk, holding a flower garland.

12. **Kaṭakāmukha (कटकामुख)**

    * *Formation:* Index and middle fingers join the thumb tip, with the ring and little fingers raised (or variants joining the ring finger).

    * *Significance:* Picking flowers, holding a pearl necklace, stringing a bow.

13. **Sūcī (सूची)**

    * *Formation:* Middle, ring, and little fingers touch the thumb; index finger is extended straight upward.

    * *Significance:* The number one, pointing, the Supreme Soul (Parabrahma), needle, world.

14. **Candrakalā (चन्द्रकला)**

    * *Formation:* From Sūcī, the thumb is released and extended sideways into an L-shape.

    * *Significance:* The digit of the moon, Shiva’s crescent ornament, face.

15. **Padmakośa (पद्मकोश)**

    * *Formation:* All fingers cupped and curved together like a bowl/hollow sphere.

    * *Significance:* Lotus bud, ball, round fruit, egg, mango.

16. **Sarpaśīrṣa (सर्पशीर्ष)**

    * *Formation:* Fingers together as in Patāka, but cupped slightly forward at the palm.

    * *Significance:* Hood of a serpent, sprinkling water, offering libations (tarpana).

17. **Mṛgaśīrṣa (मृगशीर्ष)**

    * *Formation:* Thumb, index, and middle finger touch at tips (or middle/ring bend to touch the thumb); index and little finger extended upward.

    * *Significance:* Deer's head, Krishna's flute, costume, umbrella, calling someone.

18. **Siṃhamukha (सिंहमुख)**

    * *Formation:* Middle and ring fingers touch the tip of the thumb; index and little fingers point straight upward.

    * *Significance:* Lion's face, elephant, hare, performing a homa (ritual fire).

19. **Kāṅgula / Lāṅgula (काङ्गुल)**

    * *Formation:* Ring finger folded inward to touch the palm; index, middle, and thumb curved slightly; little finger raised.

    * *Significance:* Small bell, betel nut, water lily, coconut.

20. **Alapadma (अलपद्म)**

    * *Formation:* All fingers extended, separated, and curved individually backward starting from the little finger.

    * *Significance:* Fully blossomed lotus, full moon, beauty, wheel, castle.

21. **Catura (चतुर)**

    * *Formation:* Thumb placed inside the base of the middle finger; remaining four fingers extended flat and close together.

    * *Significance:* Musk, gold, cleverness, eye expressions, small measure.

22. **Bhramara (भ्रमर)**

    * *Formation:* Index finger curled into the base of the thumb; thumb tip touches the middle finger tip; ring and pinky fingers raised.

    * *Significance:* Bumblebee, wing, cuckoo, parrot.

23. **Haṃsāsya (हंसास्य)**

    * *Formation:* Tips of the thumb and index finger touch gently; remaining three fingers extended straight.

    * *Significance:* Swan's beak, pearl, tying a thread, painting, soft touch.

24. **Haṃsapakṣa (हंसपक्ष)**

    * *Formation:* Little finger extended upward; remaining three fingers spread and relaxed sideways; thumb held close.

    * *Significance:* Swan's wing, the number six, bridge, covering.

25. **Saṃdaṃśa (संदंश)**

    * *Formation:* Fingers of Padmakośa repeatedly opened and closed together toward the thumb tip.

    * *Significance:* Pincers, plucking flowers, fear, generous giving.

26. **Mukula (मुकुल)**

    * *Formation:* All five fingertips brought together into a point pointing upward.

    * *Significance:* Blossom/bud, eating food, five arrows of Kamadeva, holding a jewel.

27. **Tāmracūḍa (ताम्रचूड)**

    * *Formation:* From Mukula or Muṣṭi, the index finger is bent like a hook.

    * *Significance:* Rooster/cock's comb, crane, crow, writing letters.

28. **Triśūla (त्रिशूल)**

    * *Formation:* Thumb holds down the little finger against the palm; index, middle, and ring fingers extended vertically.

    * *Significance:* Lord Shiva’s trident (Trishula), trinity (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva), the number three.
